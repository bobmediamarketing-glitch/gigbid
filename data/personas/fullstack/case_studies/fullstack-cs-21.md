# Team OKR tracker for a travel client

**Date:** 2023-01-04  
**Client industry:** travel  
**Role:** sole developer

## Problem
The client's team was doing this work by hand and it did not scale. They needed a reliable system they could own.

## What I built
I delivered the project using postgres, next.js, together with a type-safe ORM layer; a component-based single-page frontend; subscriptions, invoices and card payments.

## Result
The project lifted conversion by 11%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
