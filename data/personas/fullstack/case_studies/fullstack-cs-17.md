# Headless sneaker store for a education client

**Date:** 2024-09-14  
**Client industry:** education  
**Role:** sole developer

## Problem
The client's team was doing this work by hand and it did not scale. They needed a reliable system they could own.

## What I built
I delivered the project using tailwind, node.js, together with a component-based single-page frontend; server-rendered pages with file-based routing; subscriptions, invoices and card payments.

## Result
The project lifted conversion by 12%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
