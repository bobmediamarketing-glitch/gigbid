# Headless sneaker store for a legal client

**Date:** 2025-11-20  
**Client industry:** legal  
**Role:** sole developer

## Problem
The client's team was doing this work by hand and it did not scale. They needed subscription billing with trials and coupons.

## What I built
I delivered the project using stripe, react, together with utility-first styling; a relational database with migrations.

## Result
The project reached 96% accuracy on a held-out set. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
