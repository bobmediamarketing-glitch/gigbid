# Team OKR tracker for a fintech client

**Date:** 2025-04-22  
**Client industry:** fintech  
**Role:** sole developer

## Problem
The client's team was doing this work by hand and it did not scale. They needed a reliable system they could own.

## What I built
I delivered the project using react, stripe, together with server-rendered pages with file-based routing; a relational database with migrations.

## Result
The project lifted conversion by 4%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
