# Ad spend warehouse for a real estate client

**Date:** 2023-02-19  
**Client industry:** real estate  
**Role:** sole developer

## Problem
The client's team was doing this work by hand and it did not scale. They needed clean, tested models on top of raw Shopify exports.

## What I built
I delivered the project using dbt, snowflake, together with Google's serverless warehouse; scheduled DAGs that orchestrate nightly loads; hand-written analytical queries with window functions.

## Result
The project reached 88% accuracy on a held-out set. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
