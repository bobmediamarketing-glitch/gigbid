# Pricing elasticity analysis for a legal client

**Date:** 2025-02-15  
**Client industry:** legal  
**Role:** sole developer

## Problem
The client's team was doing this work by hand and it did not scale. They needed a reliable system they could own.

## What I built
I delivered the project using bigquery, postgres, together with dataframe wrangling in notebooks; hypothesis tests and confidence intervals; hand-written analytical queries with window functions.

## Result
The project reached 90% accuracy on a held-out set. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
