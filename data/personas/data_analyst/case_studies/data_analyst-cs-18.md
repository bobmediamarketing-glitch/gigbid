# Cohort retention study for a saas client

**Date:** 2025-11-28  
**Client industry:** saas  
**Role:** sole developer

## Problem
The client's team was doing this work by hand and it did not scale. They needed a reliable system they could own.

## What I built
I delivered the project using bigquery, together with dataframe wrangling in notebooks; hand-written analytical queries with window functions.

## Result
The project reduced latency from 6s to 859ms. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
