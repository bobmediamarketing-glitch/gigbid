# Parking occupancy counter for a saas client

**Date:** 2026-03-24  
**Client industry:** saas  
**Role:** sole developer

## Problem
The client's team was doing this work by hand and it did not scale. They needed cleaning up scanned receipts before OCR.

## What I built
I delivered the project using opencv, computer vision, together with Amazon cloud hosting; training custom neural networks on GPUs; containerised services.

## Result
The project lifted conversion by 22%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
