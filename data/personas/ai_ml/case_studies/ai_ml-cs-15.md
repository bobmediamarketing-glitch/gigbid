# Resume entity extractor for a marketing client

**Date:** 2026-01-11  
**Client industry:** marketing  
**Role:** sole developer

## Problem
The client's team was doing this work by hand and it did not scale. They needed a reliable system they could own.

## What I built
I delivered the project using pytorch, nlp, together with adapting a base model on the client's labelled examples; scripting the whole pipeline end to end; open-weight transformer checkpoints.

## Result
The project reached 92% accuracy on a held-out set. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
