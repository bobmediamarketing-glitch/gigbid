# Ticket intent classifier for a real estate client

**Date:** 2023-04-03  
**Client industry:** real estate  
**Role:** sole developer

## Problem
The client's team was doing this work by hand and it did not scale. They needed a reliable system they could own.

## What I built
I delivered the project using python, nlp, together with training custom neural networks on GPUs; adapting a base model on the client's labelled examples.

## Result
The project reached 97% accuracy on a held-out set. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
