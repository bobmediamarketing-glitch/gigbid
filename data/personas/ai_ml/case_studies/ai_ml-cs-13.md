# Parking occupancy counter for a logistics client

**Date:** 2024-05-30  
**Client industry:** logistics  
**Role:** sole developer

## Problem
The client's team was doing this work by hand and it did not scale. They needed a reliable system they could own.

## What I built
I delivered the project using python, aws, together with detecting objects in camera frames; training custom neural networks on GPUs.

## Result
The project reached 91% accuracy on a held-out set. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
