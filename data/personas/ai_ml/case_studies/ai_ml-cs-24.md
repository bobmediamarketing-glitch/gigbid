# Demand forecast pipeline for a legal client

**Date:** 2024-11-16  
**Client industry:** legal  
**Role:** sole developer

## Problem
The client's team was doing this work by hand and it did not scale. They needed a reliable system they could own.

## What I built
I delivered the project using fastapi, together with automated retraining and model monitoring; containerised services.

## Result
The project reached 96% accuracy on a held-out set. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
