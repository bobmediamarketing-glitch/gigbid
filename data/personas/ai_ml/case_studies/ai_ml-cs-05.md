# Resume entity extractor for a education client

**Date:** 2026-05-27  
**Client industry:** education  
**Role:** sole developer

## Problem
The client's team was doing this work by hand and it did not scale. They needed a classifier trained on our 40k labelled tickets.

## What I built
I delivered the project using fine-tuning, huggingface, together with training custom neural networks on GPUs; extracting entities and intent from free text; scripting the whole pipeline end to end.

## Result
The project cut manual effort by 74%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
