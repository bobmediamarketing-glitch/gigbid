# Contract search copilot for a e-commerce client

**Date:** 2024-08-01  
**Client industry:** e-commerce  
**Role:** sole developer

## Problem
The client's team was doing this work by hand and it did not scale. They needed a reliable system they could own.

## What I built
I delivered the project using python, fastapi, together with a self-hosted vector database; extracting entities and intent from free text; answering questions from the client's own documents with citations.

## Result
The project reduced latency from 8s to 850ms. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
