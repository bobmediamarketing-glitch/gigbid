# Medical note de-identifier for a insurance client

**Date:** 2024-04-04  
**Client industry:** insurance  
**Role:** sole developer

## Problem
The client's team was doing this work by hand and it did not scale. They needed a reliable system they could own.

## What I built
I delivered the project using nlp, huggingface, together with training custom neural networks on GPUs; adapting a base model on the client's labelled examples.

## Result
The project lifted conversion by 22%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
